# PlumeNav research wiki

PlumeNav is a two-dimensional gas-source-localisation workbench. The survey
planning research track asks whether geometry, weather, uncertain emissions and
sensor limits can predict the probability that a robotic survey will succeed,
and help select where and when to sample.

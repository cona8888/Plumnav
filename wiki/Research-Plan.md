# Research plan and roadmap

The [canonical research plan](https://github.com/cona8888/Plumnav/blob/main/docs/survey-planning/research-plan.md)
defines geometry inputs, environmental uncertainty, sensor characteristics and
source-location priors. Pipe alignments inform possible leak locations;
distributed emissions are a separate scenario that needs justification.

| Work package | Period | Issue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Geometry and baseline surveys | Months 1–3 | [SP1 / #2](https://github.com/cona8888/Plumnav/issues/2) |
| Ensembles and feasibility prediction | Months 4–6 | [SP2 / #3](https://github.com/cona8888/Plumnav/issues/3) |
| Route optimisation and held-out comparisons | Months 7–9 | [SP3 / #4](https://github.com/cona8888/Plumnav/issues/4) |
| Controlled validation and reporting | Months 10–12 | [SP4 / #5](https://github.com/cona8888/Plumnav/issues/5) |

The start date is unconfirmed. The
[project hub](https://github.com/cona8888/Plumnav/blob/main/docs/survey-planning/README.md)
records current status and dependencies. Low predicted detection probability
is conditional on the stated assumptions and does not establish absence of gas.

# Validation and experiments

The [canonical validation plan](https://github.com/cona8888/Plumnav/blob/main/docs/survey-planning/validation-plan.md)
separates numerical verification, surrogate evaluation and controlled physical
validation.

Check units, geometry transformations, source isolation, sensor response and
numerical convergence before scaling. Evaluate new models on held-out geometry
and environmental conditions, including weak emissions and no-emission controls.
Compare route strategies against systematic coverage under equal mission
budgets and paired scenarios.

Report detection probability and calibration, false alarms, localisation error,
survey duration, runtime and abstentions. Keep gas detection, concentration-peak
estimation, reinspection flags and physical source attribution distinct.

The [existing README](https://github.com/cona8888/Plumnav/blob/main/README.md) and
[open PR #1](https://github.com/cona8888/Plumnav/pull/1) record earlier work and
limitations. No scientific run or controlled trial is established by this
documentation update.

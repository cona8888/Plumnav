- Duration: 12 months; project start date unconfirmed.
- Manual annual estimate: **US$4,923.99**.
- Proposed rounded credit request: **US$4,924**; ceiling **US$5,000**.
- Pricing region: Iowa, `us-central1`; USD, on-demand, before tax and free-tier deductions.
- Calculator status: no updated saved estimate or share link has been produced.
- Award and cloud deployment: unconfirmed.

Compute Engine and Batch are proposed for dispersion ensembles and model
training, Cloud Storage for research data, and Cloud Run for the planning
prototype. Workload quantities are allowances to refine using measured runtimes.

Google Maps Platform costs are outside the research-credit request. Simulation
geometry must come from data licensed for modelling; Google Maps supplies the
visualisation layer under its applicable terms. The funding record links to
the official programme limitations, map policies and service prices.
